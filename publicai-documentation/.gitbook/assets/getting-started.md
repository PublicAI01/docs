# Getting Started with DataHunter

Welcome to DataHunter! This guide walks you through posting your first reward-eligible comment. The whole flow takes about a minute once your account is set up.

---

## Step 1 — Open DataHunter and connect your account

Click the DataHunter icon in your Chrome toolbar. You'll see a welcome screen with the **Connect Account** button.

<!-- DROP IN IMAGE: 01-connect-account.png -->
![DataHunter welcome screen](images/01-connect-account.png)

Click **Connect Account** and sign in with your DataHub credentials. If you don't have a DataHub account yet, you'll need to [create one first](#).

{% hint style="info" %}
DataHunter remembers you across browser sessions — you'll only connect once.
{% endhint %}

---

## Step 2 — Open AI Reply from the popup

After signing in, the DataHunter popup shows your **Today's Comments** counter (e.g. `0/25`) and a short summary of the reward model.

<!-- DROP IN IMAGE: 02-popup-dashboard.png -->
![DataHunter popup dashboard](images/02-popup-dashboard.png)

Click the purple **AI Reply** button. This opens X in a new tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you're already logged into X in your browser, you'll go straight to your timeline. If not, X will prompt you to log in — use your normal X credentials.
{% endhint %}

---

## Step 3 — Find a post worth replying to

Pick a post that fits the campaign. Good candidates:

* **Trading, crypto, or AI agent topics** — these match what Agentica is about
* **News-worthy posts** — partnerships, launches, market events
* **Posts with 1,000+ views** — these are the only ones eligible for rewards

Avoid replying to posts about politics, drama, tragedies, or anything off-topic. Off-topic comments don't help the campaign and won't earn rewards.

When you find a good post, click **Reply** as you normally would on X.

---

## Step 4 — Pick a voice

Once the reply box is open, you'll see a pink **Select Reply** button next to X's native Reply button. Click it.

<!-- DROP IN IMAGE: 03-voice-picker.png -->
![Voice picker dropdown](images/03-voice-picker.png)

A small menu drops down with four voices:

| Voice | Tone |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🔥 **Hype** | Enthusiastic, amplifying |
| 🤔 **Skeptic** | Curious, asks a question |
| 💡 **Insider** | Drops context |
| 👋 **Casual** | Low-key, relatable |

Pick the voice that best fits the post. DataHunter generates a comment in that voice and fills it into the reply box automatically.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Tip:** Want a different version? Click **Select Reply** again and pick another voice — or pick the same voice again for a new variant.
{% endhint %}

---

## Step 5 — Review and post

Your generated comment appears in the reply box, sometimes including hashtags or mentions like `#agentica` or `@agenticatrade`.

<!-- DROP IN IMAGE: 04-posted-reply.png -->
![Generated reply in X composer](images/04-posted-reply.png)

**Before you post:**

* Read the comment to make sure it fits the conversation
* Light edits are fine — tweak wording, adjust tone, fix anything that feels off
* Make sure you're following [X's community guidelines](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-rules) — avoid restricted topics

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Note about tags:** Sometimes the AI generates a comment without `#agentica` or `@agenticatrade`. This is intentional — the system varies tagging so your replies look organic on X. The comment still counts toward your rewards.
{% endhint %}

When you're happy with the comment, click X's black **Reply** button to post it.

---

## What happens next

Your comment will be verified within about 60 seconds. Once verified:

* Your **Today's Comments** counter ticks up by 1
* You earn one **share** of this month's reward pool
* The comment counts toward your monthly payout

You can post up to **25 comments per day**. Rewards are paid out at the end of each month in **$PUBLIC** or **USDT**.
