## What is AI Arena - Chat Feature? <a href="#id-1b6e" id="id-1b6e"></a>

Generative AI has become widely known, but different AI models have varying capabilities. For example, ChatGPT excels at copywriting, Claude is more rational, Gemini is better at personified emotional expressions, etc.

What if you could ask the same question to several AI models simultaneously? Who would provide the best answer? This is the functionality that AI Arena allows you to achieve.

You can rank the quality of answers through a simple drag-and-drop interaction to earn points, or you can do nothing and just chat with AI - **it's completely free**.

## What is AI Arena - Data Feature? <a href="#id-1b6e" id="id-1b6e"></a>

Training AI models requires high-quality data, and currently, mainstream AI models have almost exhausted all the data that can be crawled from the internet.

However, there is a neglected corner: **private domain data**. Telegram has many high-quality channels containing current news, insightful perspectives, and even official messages. These are very useful for training a distinctive AI model. We want to rely on users to collect this type of information and provide certain rewards, achieving a win-win situation through cryptocurrency.
