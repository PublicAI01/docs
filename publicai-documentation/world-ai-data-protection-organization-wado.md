# 🌎 World AI Data Protection Organization (WADO)

#### Background

As many of you know, the recent Reddit data sale has generated 60 million dollars without benefiting the original content creators.

[https://cointelegraph.com/news/reddit-faces-ftc-probe-over-ai-data-practices-ahead-of-ipo](https://cointelegraph.com/news/reddit-faces-ftc-probe-over-ai-data-practices-ahead-of-ipo)

**World AI Data Protection Organization (WADO)** is a nonprofit organization, pioneering initiative dedicated to safeguarding the rights and interests of individuals contributing data towards the development of AI. Aims to lead efforts in protecting AI training data ownership through collaboration, research, and media advocacy. WADO envisions a future where every AI contributor is fairly recognized and compensated for their data and establishes a global standard for ethical AI data use, promoting transparency and equity.
