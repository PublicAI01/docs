# ✅ Voting on Quality

## The Voting Process: How and Why It Works

Voting is the heart of the PublicAI validation process. It ensures that the data uploaded to the ecosystem meets the highest standards, creating a foundation for AI that is accurate, inclusive, and impactful. Here’s how it works and why it matters:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (67).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Why Stake?

To participate in voting, you must stake $PUBLIC points. This stake acts as a measure of your commitment to quality and accountability. The amount you stake is influenced by your **reputation**:

* **High Reputation:** Requires only minimal stakes, reflecting your proven track record.
* **Low Reputation:** Requires higher stakes, as a safeguard against poor-quality contributions.

Staking ensures that validators are incentivized to contribute thoughtfully and avoid careless decisions.

## **What Happens During Voting?**

1. **Submit Your Vote:** Review the data samples provided, assess their quality, and cast your vote.
2. **Reach Consensus:** Once enough validators participate, a consensus is determined based on majority agreement.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (68).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## **What You Earn (or Lose):**

* **If You’re in Consensus:**
  * **Stake Returned:** Your commitment pays off.
  * **Reputation Increased:** Your credibility as a validator grows.
  * **Reward Earned:** You gain additional $PUBLIC points as a token of appreciation for your quality contribution.
* **If You’re Out of Consensus:**
  * **Stake Lost:** A signal that your judgment was not aligned with the majority.
  * **Reputation Decreased:** Reflecting the need to improve accuracy.
  * **No Reward:** Only validators in consensus earn rewards.

## **What If You Disagree with the Outcome?**

Mistakes happen, and we value your dedication to ensuring the system is fair. If you believe your vote was accurate despite being out of consensus:

* **Open a Ticket:** Reach out to our support team at [discord.gg/publicai](https://discord.gg/publicai).
* **Earn Elevated Privileges:** Demonstrating your eye for quality and commitment could earn you a place in the **PublicAI Guards**—a role of increased responsibility, earning potential, and trust within the ecosystem.

## Why This System Works

The PublicAI voting process rewards meaningful contributions while discouraging low-quality or harmful actions. Validators who provide **valuable signals** are rewarded with higher reputation, minimal staking requirements, and better earnings. Conversely, those who introduce **noise** face penalties, protecting the ecosystem and ensuring its integrity.

By participating thoughtfully, you’re helping to build a smarter, more equitable AI future while earning real rewards for your expertise.
