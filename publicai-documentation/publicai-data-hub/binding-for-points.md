# ⛓️ Binding for Points

After logging in, increase your earning potential and unlock exclusive opportunities by connecting your social accounts and binding a wallet. Each connected account earns you **$PUBLIC points**, helping you get started and making your contributions even more valuable.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (63).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Binding Your Wallet

Binding a wallet is essential for claiming **USDC payouts** and preparing for future **$PUBLIC point conversions**. We support wallets on **Solana**, **Near Protocol**, and **BNB** chains, offering secure and flexible options for all users.

## Expanding Your Network

Link your **Telegram, Discord, X, LinkedIn**, and other social accounts to establish your credentials as an expert in the PublicAI ecosystem. These connections not only earn you additional **$PUBLIC points** but also unlock access to exclusive campaigns and opportunities tailored to your expertise and engagement.

## Why Connect?

* **Earn Points Instantly:** Each connected wallet and social account earns you $PUBLIC points, increasing your rewards from day one.
* **Credentialing Your Expertise:** Verified social accounts help showcase your expertise and position you for specialized opportunities.
* **Unlock Exclusive Opportunities:** Gain access to unique campaigns, roles, and perks by connecting your accounts.
* **Prepare for Future Payouts:** Wallet binding ensures secure management of your $PUBLIC points and enables USDC payouts.
